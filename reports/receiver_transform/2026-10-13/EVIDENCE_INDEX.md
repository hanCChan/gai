# Evidence index

- [evidence.zip](evidence.zip): 124 CRC/hash-verified members; complete configurations, 400 training epochs, 20 fitted run statuses, 8 zero-step launch aborts and source snapshots.
- [evidence_members.json](evidence_members.json): byte lengths and SHA256 for every ZIP member.
- [training_jobs.csv](training_jobs.csv): checkpoint/feature/initialization/schedule hashes; no weights or features.
- [held_TX_RX_pair_distances.csv](held_TX_RX_pair_distances.csv): all3200 measurements.
- [input_provenance.csv](input_provenance.csv): original source IQ and frozen row/label/cache hashes.
- [pre_execution_seal.json](pre_execution_seal.json), [implementation_repair.json](implementation_repair.json), [verification.json](verification.json): pre-fit seal, disclosed implementation repair and independent decision recomputation.

Raw IQ, numeric feature tensors, model weights, credentials, private manuscripts and correspondence are excluded.
